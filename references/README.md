## Closed access, and how a copy gets here

*Byron, September 19, 2026: "One reason we are putting references in walnutbutter_secret is, although
they are part of the recipe, some of our references will by necessity be closed-access references. I am
able to use institutional libraries on a limited basis for article requests. I will be acceding these
copies as I acquire them."*

So the folder has two kinds of copy, and the table says which is which:

- **Freely available** -- fetched from the source named, which is a publisher's open access, an author's
  own page, a repository, or the Internet Archive's capture of one. Nothing from a paywall or a pirate
  mirror. This is the only kind a script fetches.
- **Acquired by Byron** -- through an institutional library request, on a limited budget of requests, or
  bought. These are why this repository is private: they are part of the recipe, but they are not ours to
  redistribute, and only this README is tracked in the public checkout.

A paper that is closed today therefore stays in "Not in the folder" with an issue open against it, as a
standing request list. When Byron acquires one, its bullet becomes a row whose source column says so.
