**Why the clause gives zero at the read.**
- The score on synapse k→i at a read is a sum over the epoch's waves: gate × entry × trace. The gate is V_i > 0 and m > 0; the entry is a·c − (F−a)·m; the trace is x_ki.
- The notes, settles and re-bases are an exact rewrite of that sum. Each open arrival collects the gain now minus the gain when it arrived, which is every entry posted from its own wave on.
- Everything multiplying the entry is fixed before the wave's fresh draws: the gate, the trace, m and F. Arrivals, the floor and spikes all come before the draws.
- Given that, the number of escapes is Binomial(F, 1 − e^−m). The entry's mean is F(1 − e^−m)·c − F·e^−m·m = 0.
- So each wave adds a zero-mean term, and the sum over an epoch has mean exactly zero. The V > 0 gate, the floor and discharge clears, the re-base at the read, and any link between x and later gates only choose which zero-mean terms enter.
- At mnist's point it is stronger still. The outputs' only synapse is the read synapse, whose escapes deliver nothing, so the epoch runs the same whatever those draws are. The sum therefore has mean zero given the whole run, and its conditional variance is Σ X²·m²·e^−m/(1 − e^−m). The square root of its mean is 15.4–17.6, against observed sds of 15.5–17.3.

**Why +0.36 turned up.** Each epoch is about 38 rare escape credits, each around X ≈ 1.45, against a steady small debit. The distribution leans right and its mean rides on the top 1% of epochs.
