**Verdict: I agree, (c).** The +0.35 is noise. The engine follows §8.16, and under the clause the summed eligibility has mean exactly zero at every read. I checked this with my own scripts and 2.5 CPU-hours. Nothing is still running.

**1. The expectation, re-derived from §8.16**
- Given the state before the wave's draws, the number of escapes is Binomial(F, P), with P = 1 − e^−m and c = m e^−m / P.
- The mean of a·c − (F − a)·m is F·P·c − F·e^−m·m = F·m·e^−m − F·m·e^−m = 0.
- Computed exactly at 60 digits for m from 1e-9 to 50 and F of 1, 2, 5 and 24, the mean is at most 3e-60 of F·m. In the engine's float forms it is at most 1.9e-16 of F·m. The variance is F·m²·e^−m / P, confirmed to 40 digits.
- A brute-force check of 1e8 draws at m of 0.001, 0.0036 and 0.02 gives z of +1.58, +2.05 and −1.63.
- The gate (V > 0 and m > 0), the trace, m and F are all fixed before the wave's draws, so each wave adds a zero-mean term. At mnist's point the outputs' read escapes deliver nothing, so an epoch's sum has mean zero given the whole run.

**4. What the first check missed or got wrong**
- **The skew argument does not explain the +0.36.**
  - At n = 14,000 the mean is effectively normal. A bootstrap of the critic's pool, centred on zero, gives a skew of the mean of +0.015 and P(mean ≥ +0.362) = 0.0019, where the normal curve gives 0.0017.
  - Dropping the top 1% of epochs pulls down the mean of any right-skewed sample whose mean is zero; here it removes 0.47. That the top 1% carries the mean is therefore not evidence of anything.
  - The +0.36 is simply about a 1-in-300 event (two-sided), met among the many numbers in the critic's report. The case for noise rests on the exact derivation, the per-wave checks and the replications, not on the skew.
- **The resume path was never tested.** Two of the critic's four records were continued from checkpoints. All twelve of the first check's replications were fresh, and none of its objects-against-Rust comparisons ran on a resumed network. Sections 2 and 3 cover that path now, and it is clean.
